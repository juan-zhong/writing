Love That Never Starts

2024 · 10 · 英文原作

I don’t like him

being so straightrealisticand arrogant

But I dohave a feeling towards himwhich I find irresistibleand unexplainable

When I walk alongside the Seinein a latesnowy nightI think about him

When I stand on the shore of the Mediterranean Seawatching sunrisein an earlychilling morningI think about him

When I sit on the plane back to the cityhaving no idea what time it is and on top of which country I amI think about him

I missthe cool summerthe freezing winterand the warm springwe spent together

I missthe long walksthe endless drinksthe indie playliststhe childhood storiesand even the tattoos we both have for our exes

I missthe films and cinemasthe tears and laughterthe notification sounds of the messages
