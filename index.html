随笔

求学期间，我有幸接受了人文社科领域内不同学科的学术训练。这些学习经历和我的日常生活经验，让我保持了思考和持续写作的习惯。因此我在学术论文之外也创作了一些诗歌、散文、杂记、和翻译，于是整理成了这部随笔选集。

我写作时多用中文，有时也用英文，偶尔也做一些翻译。整理成册的过程中，我为各篇作品确定了中英双语标题，可以通过下方的中文或英文目录查看，各篇正文内容均依照当时的写作语言呈现。

A Collection ofWRITINGS

Over the years, I was lucky to receive academic training in various fields across the humanities and social sciences. My formal studies and life experiences helped me keep thinking and writing. This site collects some of my written works beyond academic papers, including poems, essays, notes, and translations.

I write mostly in Chinese, sometmes in English, and occasionally I translate in between. The table of contents can be viewed in Chinese or English, with my own edited trnaslation of titles of the each peice, while each piece is presented in the language in they way I wrote originally.
