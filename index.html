书写选集

求学期间，我有幸接受了不同领域的学术训练，涵盖人类学、历史学、文学、政治学、社会学、哲学和心理学等学科。虽然我目前主攻语言学，我对其他领域依旧保持着浓厚的兴趣。这个页面收录了语言学之外的文字作品，包括诗歌，散文，随笔，杂记，翻译，笔记等。因为平时使用多种语言，所以我的写作有中文的也有英文的，翻译有英译中也有中译英。读者可以选择中文或英文查看目录。

Over the years, I was fortunate to receive academic training across various fields, including anthropology, history, literature, political science, sociology, philosophy and psychology. Although my main field is now linguistics, I remain deeply interested in these other disciplines. This page collects my writing beyond linguistics: poems, prose, essays, miscellanies, translations and notes. Because I use several languages in daily life, I write in both Chinese and English, and I translate in both directions, from English into Chinese and from Chinese into English. Readers may view the contents in either Chinese or English.
