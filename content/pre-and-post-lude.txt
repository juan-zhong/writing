All these blank pages before me, nine years left on the shelf
Not relegated to decoration gathering dust, but to revisited by my forthcoming self
In aftershocks, fluttered into floorboard creases
Limp flags restless to emblemize
Uncharted lands, a collage of snapshots 
Yearning to be cherished come hindsight 
Delving into a profusion of tongues, cousins to the Nth removed
I hope to train my mind to syncretize
A balance between all the holes 
That pull me taught enough to gather pollen from all the strangers on my path
That’ll open me to unimaginable apertures 
Soaking sun
To a degree unmet in the past
