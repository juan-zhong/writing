I don’t like him

being so straight
realistic
and arrogant

But I do 
have a feeling towards him
which I find irresistible
and unexplainable

When I walk alongside the Seine
in a late
snowy night
I think about him

When I stand on the shore of the Mediterranean Sea
watching sunrise
in an early 
chilling morning
I think about him

When I sit on the plane back to the city
having no idea what time it is and on top of which country I am
I think about him

I miss 
the cool summer
the freezing winter
and the warm spring
spend together

I miss 
the long walks
the endless drinks
the inde playlists
the childhood stories
and even the tattoos we both have for our exes

I miss
the films and cinemas
the tears and laughters
the notification sounds of the messages

I miss
the first night we went out
and it was like
I’ve known him for years

I love him
finally
 I am able to say this

We were not together
we are not together
I suppose
we will not be together
either

Maybe
some love is supposed not to be
started

I don’t know 
how to deal with my wild thoughts

So I paint
my memories
into
shades
and shapes 
on canvas
